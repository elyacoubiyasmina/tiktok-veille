## 1. Burnout Recovery / Rebuilding After Depression

### Top Creators 500K+ (2026)
- **@drjulie (Dr. Julie Smith)** — ~5M. UK clinical psychologist, NHS-trained. Short CBT/burnout coping ("what to do when you can't get out of bed").
- **@nadiaaddesi** — ~2.8M. Canadian social worker. Trauma recovery, ADHD, "healing the version of you that survived."
- **@therapyjeff** — ~2.8M. Attachment styles + depression recovery scripts.
- **@micheline.maalouf** — ~1.1M. Florida trauma therapist, PTSD survivor. Burnout + nervous system.
- **@elysemyers** — ~1.5M. Storytelling-format anxiety/depression diaries — huge women 25-35 base.
- **@herhealingera** — healing-era POV vlogs (viral handle).
- **FR (under-saturated):** Morgane Hevasia — ex-burnout coach, far smaller than US peers → open lane.

### Verbatim Hooks (first 3s)
- "POV: you're rebuilding your life after losing yourself to burnout."
- "Day 1 of healing: I chose silence — not because I didn't care, but because I finally did."
- "Burnout doesn't look like falling apart in public. It happens in silence — here's mine."
- "Nobody told me recovery from depression would look this boring."

- "I quit my job because no salary is worth crying in the bathroom — month 1 vs month 6."
- "Things I wish I knew before my breakdown at 28."
- "It takes 1 to 3 years to recover from burnout — here's where I am."

### Comment + Share Triggers
- **Permission-to-rest** framing ("you're not lazy, you're depleted").
- **Boring-recovery realism** vs aesthetic — anti-performance drives saves.
- **Timeline POVs** (Day 1 → Day 90) → "stitch with your version."
- **Silent breakdown** narrative — high-functioning depression resonates with employed women 25-35.
- Background music + voiceover outperforms talking-head (JMIR 2026).

### FR Gaps
- "Le burn-out silencieux" day-in-the-life series — FR still treats burnout as HR topic, not vlog.
- **Nervous system regulation in French** — huge US/UK, absent FR.
- **Post-arrêt-maladie comeback diaries** — unique FR social-sécu context (arrêt, RQTH) nobody documents.
- "Soft life" in French — no FR creator owns it.
- Couples-rebuilding-after-her-burnout angle — empty.
- Burnout-recovery-at-30 as **identity content** (not clinical advice).

### Verbatim Comments

## Cross-Niche Synthesis

**Universal 2026 winning patterns:**
1. **Raw > polished** — delulu/aesthetic is dead, vulnerability wins.
2. **Specificity > vague** — exact ages, exact day numbers, exact rituals.
3. **Permission-giving language** outperforms aspirational.
4. **POV + overlay text** = highest stitch/duet rate.
5. **Day-numbered series** (Day 1 → Day X) drive binge + return.
6. **Nervous-system / therapy-coded vocabulary** legitimizes emotion.

**Biggest open FR lanes (rank-ordered):**
1. Divorce-effect / starting-over-at-30 vulnerability (zero FR creator owns it).
2. Shadow work + inner-child journaling FR (#shadowwork = 2.3B EN views, near-zero FR).
3. Nervous-system regulation in plain French.
4. "75 Moyen" / softer challenge formats with FR habit-stack tracker.
5. Post-arrêt-maladie / burnout comeback diaries (unique FR social-sécu context).
6. Hot Girl Walk FR chapters.

**Format to test first (FR):**
Talking-head, bare-faced, day-numbered series → "Jour X de ma reconstruction après burnout" with overlay text + emo audio. Hits 4 of 6 universal patterns at once.
